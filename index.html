The Gainesville Inland Port Opportunity

The Gainesville Inland Port connects Northeast Georgia manufacturers directly to Savannah's deepwater port via intermodal rail. For companies shipping high volumes to coastal or international destinations, this represents a potential shift from over-the-road trucking to cost-effective rail. The question isn't whether the port exists—it's whether your freight profile justifies the transition. That's where a Port Readiness Assessment provides clarity.
