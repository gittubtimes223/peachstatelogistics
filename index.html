Navigating the Gainesville Short-Haul Rail Corridor

The Gainesville Inland Port connects Northeast Georgia directly to Savannah’s Garden City Terminal via Norfolk Southern rail. While the sustainability and long-haul cost models are clear, short-haul intermodal introduces complex friction points—including fixed train schedules, container yard dwell times, and local drayage logistics. The question isn't whether the infrastructure exists; it’s whether your specific container volume and lead-time tolerance justify the transition. Our assessments provide the exact lane data needed to safeguard your line velocity before altering your carrier routing.
